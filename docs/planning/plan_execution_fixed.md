## Current Status (as of April 9, 2026)

- **Project:** Sectorbound Voxel RPG
- **Goal:** Achieve the vertical slice described in PLAN.md (land, gather, build, fight, skill up, defeat boss, persist, reload, sector transition)

## Key Choices and Progress

- **Sector/Planet Transitions:** Fully implemented. Press 'N' to warp to a new sector (loads 3 new planets, persists state).
- **Mining & Building:** Mining (Space/Left Click) and block placement (Enter/Right Click) work as intended. Drops go to inventory, and building requires resources.
- **Crafting:** Recipes are available and grant skill XP. Shelter and advanced crafting now require a placed workbench on the current planet, making the work area functional.
- **Combat & Boss Fights:** Fighting enemies and bosses is implemented. Defeating a boss grants loot, skill XP, and unlocks a perk.
- **Skill & Perk Progression:** Skills increase with use; perks and boss bonuses are awarded and persisted.
- **Persistence:** Saving ('S') and loading ('L') work for all relevant state (world, inventory, skills, perks, boss bonuses, etc.).
- **UI Feedback:** The sidebar/status overlay shows sector, planet, inventory, skills, perks, and vertical slice progress.
